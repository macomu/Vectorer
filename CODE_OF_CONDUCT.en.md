# Code of Conduct

**Languages:** [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | English

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Vectorer community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Examples of Expected Behavior

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

## Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical or contact details) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and Reporting

This Code of Conduct applies within all GitHub spaces associated with this repository, including Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and release comments.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it using GitHub's built-in **Report content** option on the relevant issue, pull request, or comment, or notify the maintainers through a GitHub Issue or Discussion (please avoid posting sensitive personal details publicly).
